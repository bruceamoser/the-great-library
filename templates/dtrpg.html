DriveThruRPG library



  Downloads your DriveThruRPG purchases, then de-duplicates them
  (keeping the curated shelf copy wherever new items duplicate what you already
  had), folds them into the library shelves so they sit alongside related
  material, and rebuilds the catalog. DriveThruRPG is recorded as a source,
  not kept as a separate shelf. Only new and changed files are fetched after
  the first run.
